## Information used by the app

Expressive Launcher L3 stores launcher preferences, Home-screen layout, folders, hidden-app
settings, icon choices, and related configuration on your device. Eligible app data may be
included in Android's backup system according to your device and Google account settings.

The app can access the following information only for features you choose:

- Installed applications, so it can provide Home, All Apps, search, shortcuts, widgets, and
  icon-pack functionality.
- Contacts, after permission is granted, to return matching people in launcher search.
- Images, video, audio, fonts, or other user-selected files for file search and customization.
- App usage statistics, after special access is granted, to rank on-device app predictions.
- Smartspacer content when you enable integration with the separately installed Smartspacer app.
- Accessibility global actions when you explicitly enable gesture actions. The service subscribes
  to no accessibility events and does not read content from other apps.
