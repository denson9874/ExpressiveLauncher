1. Suggestions in dock toggle (XDA-004): new switch in Home screen, Dock and Suggestions settings. Turn it off and empty dock spots stay empty; pinned apps are never moved.
2. Solid status bar background (XDA-010): new Home screen > Status bar option paints a black bar behind the clock and icons on Home and keeps the icons light so they stay readable.
3. Dock settings from Home screen settings (XDA-012): a Dock row in Home screen settings opens the full Dock page without digging into Advanced.
