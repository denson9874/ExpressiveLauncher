<b>Expressive Launcher 4.0.5 (Build 54)</b>

<b>✨ What's New & Improved</b>

1. <b>Work Profile Tab Alignment & Layout Fixes</b>
• Fixed a critical layout regression where the <b>Personal</b> and <b>Work</b> profile tab strip could be translated down by hundreds of pixels directly over app icons.
• Fixed an issue where closing the app drawer while scrolled caused the profile tabs to remain in a collapsed state and fail to reload when reopening the drawer.
• Guaranteed that whenever the app drawer is at the top, the Personal/Work tab header is immediately visible and aligned at zero translation.

2. <b>Edge-to-Edge Full-Screen App Drawer</b>
• Restored standard edge-to-edge full-screen App Drawer presentation on all handheld devices, disabling the experimental bottom sheet presentation that introduced top pull handles (<code>—</code>) and sheet margin cutoffs.

3. <b>Fast Scroll Synchronization & Stability</b>
• Restored pure Launcher3 fast scroll synchronization and position reset mechanics, eliminating invalid scroll coordinate type conversions and ensuring clean, instantaneous returns to the top of the app list.
