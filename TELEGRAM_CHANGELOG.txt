<b>Expressive Launcher 3.0.4 QA — Contrast in the Clouds</b>

Welcome to Expressive Launcher 3.0.4! Today's focused improvement directly addresses community feedback from our XDA Pixel testing threads regarding status bar contrast and setting clarity.

───────────────────────

<b>✨ What's New</b>
• <b>Clarified Dark Status Bar Controls (Community Report XDA-003)</b>:
• <b>The Issue</b>: Community members rightly pointed out that the label <i>"Dark status bar"</i> was confusing. Many users expected it to paint a dark background banner across the top of their screen, whereas the toggle actually controls icon and text contrast.
• <b>The Fix</b>: The preference label has been updated to <b>Dark status bar icons</b> with a clear subtitle: <i>"Display dark icons and clock text on the status bar for lighter wallpapers"</i>.
• Under the hood, this cleanly controls Android's <code>SYSTEM_UI_FLAG_LIGHT_STATUS_BAR</code> (via <code>SystemUiController</code>), ensuring optimal contrast without unexpected UI artifacts.
• <b>Robust Unit & Regression Coverage</b>:
• Added <code>StatusBarPreferencesTest</code> covering preference persistence, string localization, and SystemUI controller flag mapping.
• All 623 Gradle tasks, unit test suites, and 279 CI contract tests passed with zero failures.
