<b>Expressive Launcher 5.1.0 (Build 72) — Back in Settings</b>

If you're on Android 16 or older, 5.1.0 gets you back into Settings. Since 5.0.5, a build mix-up made Settings and the app Customize sheet crash on those versions. This update fixes it and adds a release check so the same mistake can't ship again. Android 17 users weren't affected, and nothing changes for them.

───────────────────────

<b>✨ What's New &amp; Improved</b>

<b>1. ⚙️ Settings opens again on Android 12 to 16</b>
• <b>Before</b>: from 5.0.5 to 5.0.9, opening Settings crashed straight away on Android 16 and older.
• <b>Now</b>: Settings opens normally.

<b>2. ✏️ The Customize sheet opens again on Android 12 to 16</b>
• <b>Before</b>: long-pressing an app and tapping Customize crashed on the same Android versions, from the same cause.
• <b>Now</b>: the sheet opens and you can rename the app and change its icon as usual. Other pop-up sheets drawn the same way share this fix.

<b>3. 🛡️ A release check for this kind of crash</b>
• Every build is now scanned for this exact mistake, and a build that has it can't be released. It would have stopped 5.0.5.

<b>💬 Community Notes</b>
• <b>Who notices</b>: anyone on Android 12 to 16 who couldn't open Settings or Customize. Android 17 users won't see a difference.
• <b>Thanks</b>: to the tester who sent in the crash logs from a vivo phone. Those two logs found the bug.
• <b>Why it slipped through</b>: our automated checks only ran on Android 17, where the crash can't happen.
• <b>Validation</b>: Jenkins passed 658 unit tests and its automated checks on an Android 17 emulator, and Settings and Customize were opened by hand on an Android 12 emulator (details below). Android 16 itself hasn't been run here, so if anything still crashes on your phone, please send a bug report.
• <b>Developer Support</b>: Expressive Pro is available as a one-time $4.99 activation. Every dollar funds the build servers, test devices and signing pipeline that keep Expressive fast, independent and ad-free.
