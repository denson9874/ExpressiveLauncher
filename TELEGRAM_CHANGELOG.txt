<b>✨ Version 3: The Next Chapter</b>

To ensure ongoing, independent development and long-term sustainability, Version 3 introduces our refined tier architecture:

• <b>Free Core (Free Forever &amp; Supported)</b>:
Choosing to remain on Free Core continues to receive regular updates, security patches, bug fixes, and 100% rock-solid Pixel Launcher parity with full Google Discover feed support. Free will always be maintained, privacy-first, and completely ad-free.

• <b>Expressive Pro (All Customization Features Gated)</b>:
All personalization and customization features across the entire launcher are now exclusive to Expressive Pro to support ongoing development:
  • <b>Workspace Grid &amp; Padding</b>: Custom column, row, and padding sliders, foldable layouts, and dock icon counts.
  • <b>App Drawer Layout &amp; Folders</b>: Custom drawer folders, category tabs, and drag-and-drop reordering.
  • <b>Dock, Gestures &amp; Folders</b>: Full dock styling, folder customization, and custom gesture mapping.
  • <b>Icon Packs &amp; Custom Shapes</b>: Per-app icon overrides, custom icon pack selection, and shape editor with individual corner sculpting.
  • <b>Typography &amp; Fonts</b>: Curated Google Sans Flex variable font and extensive typography picker.
  • <b>Hidden Apps &amp; Popup Menus</b>: App hiding management and custom launcher popup menu editor.
  • <b>Smartspacer &amp; Search Providers</b>: Enhanced Smartspacer integration and custom dock search bar providers.
