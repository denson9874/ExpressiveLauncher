<b>Expressive Launcher 3.1.3 QA — Shape Up, Scroll On</b>

Welcome to Expressive Launcher 3.1.3 (Build 45)! This one is all about settings that tell the truth: folders that finally dress like your app icons, settings pages that admit there is more below the fold, and rows that warn you before they whisk you off to Android's own settings. Every item comes straight from your XDA feedback — thank you, especially to the Pixel 11 Pro thread.

───────────────────────

<b>✨ What's New & Improved</b>

1. <b>Folders match your app icons (XDA-006)</b>
• New <b>Settings > Folders > Match app icon shape</b> toggle. Pick squircle icons and your folders go squircle too.
• It is on automatically for anyone who never chose a folder shape. If you already picked a custom folder shape, it stays exactly as you left it.
• Choosing a folder shape by hand switches matching off, so what you pick is what you get.

2. <b>Scroll hints in Settings (XDA-007)</b>
• Long settings pages now show a slim scroll thumb on the edge, so it is obvious when more options sit below the fold.
• It brightens while you scroll, stays clear of the top bar, and simply is not drawn on pages that fit on screen.

3. <b>Clear hand-offs to Android settings (XDA-008)</b>
• Rows that leave Expressive for an Android system screen — like <b>Wallpaper & style</b> and <b>Notification dots</b> — now carry an "opens elsewhere" icon.
• TalkBack announces it as <i>Opens Android settings</i>, so nobody lands in system settings by surprise.

4. <b>Honest Wallpaper & style subtitle (XDA-013)</b>
• The row used to promise an "app grid" it does not control. It now says it opens Android's Wallpaper & style for wallpaper, colors and themed icons. Grid size lives in Expressive's own Home screen settings.
