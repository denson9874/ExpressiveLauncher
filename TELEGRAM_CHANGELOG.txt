<b>Expressive Launcher QA 2.0.10 (29) — The Key to the Apps</b>

Welcome to <b>Expressive Launcher QA 2.0.10</b>! This update delivers a dedicated, accessible <b>App Drawer Icon</b> option—making opening your full app library as effortless as a single tap, especially for users who prefer classic buttons over swipe gestures.

───────────────────────

<b>🚀 What's New & Fixed</b>
• <b>App Drawer Icon Option & Direct Shortcut (<a href="https://github.com/denson9874/ExpressiveLauncher/issues/17">#17</a>)</b>:
• Many users—particularly elderly individuals, those with fine-motor difficulties, or fans of classic launcher layouts—find vertical swipe gestures tiring or inconsistent.
• Added a dedicated, 1-tap <b>App Drawer shortcut</b> (<code>AppDrawerShortcutActivity</code>) equipped with the iconic apps grid icon and seamless animation straight into All Apps.
• Added convenient pinning toggles in <b>Settings > Home Screen > General</b> and <b>Settings > Dock > Icons</b> so you can place an App Drawer button onto your home screen or dock in seconds.
• Refined launcher shortcut handling across cold and warm starts to guarantee instant, reliable activation every single time.
