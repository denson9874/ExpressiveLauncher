<b>Expressive Launcher 3.1.0 — Smart Recent Apps & Drawer Header Controls</b>

Welcome to Expressive Launcher 3.1.0 (Build 42)! This release introduces our user-requested smart recently-installed apps ranking in the drawer suggestions row, independent controls for drawer search and suggestion headers, and refined prediction row layout stability.

───────────────────────

<b>✨ What's New & Improved</b>

1. <b>Recently Installed Apps Prioritization (Closes #21)</b>:
• Added smart installation-order ranking into the app drawer prediction engine. Freshly installed or updated applications can now automatically bubble to the top of your drawer suggestions row so you can launch new apps instantly without searching.
• Configurable directly from <b>Settings > App Drawer > Suggestions</b> and <b>Settings > App Drawer > Search</b>.

2. <b>Independent Drawer Search & Suggestions Controls</b>:
• De-coupled the drawer search bar and the top suggestions row. You can now enable or disable drawer suggestions independently of the drawer search bar, giving you complete granular control over your app drawer layout.
• Added direct preference switches in both <b>App Drawer</b> settings and <b>Search</b> settings.

3. <b>Prediction Row Stability & Android 17 Private Space Parity</b>:
• Guarded prediction row layout updates (<code>mParent.onHeightUpdated()</code>) to prevent null-pointer race conditions during early drawer initialization or theme changes.
• Reinforced Private Space profile visibility boundaries to ensure secure apps never leak into prediction surfaces when Private Space is locked.
