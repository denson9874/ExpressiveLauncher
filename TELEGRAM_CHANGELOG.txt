<b>Expressive Launcher 4.0.3 QA — Folding Under Pressure</b>

Welcome to Expressive Launcher 4.0.3 (Build 52)! Large folders arrived in 4.0.2, and you told us they felt half-finished: they refused to grow next to a widget, there was no obvious way to shrink them back, and every animation still behaved like a small folder. This build reworks all three, and it adds preloaded font styles for everyone.

───────────────────────

<b>✨ What's New & Improved</b>

1. <b>Large folders can cover widgets</b> (<a href="https://xdaforums.com/t/app-qa-android-17-expressive-launcher-looking-for-pixel-11-pro-pro-xl-feedback.4801792/post-90744865">XDA request</a>)
• <b>Before:</b> making a folder large next to a widget said "No room" and nothing happened.
• <b>Now:</b> the 2x2 folder grows over the widget and sits on top of it. Taps where they overlap go to the folder; the rest of the widget keeps working. The widget never moves or resizes, and shrinking the folder uncovers it again.
• Icons, other folders and the At a Glance row still block a large folder, so nothing ever gets hidden underneath one.

2. <b>Resize folders with handles</b>
• Long-press a Home screen folder and let go without moving it: a frame with four handles appears, like the widget resize frame.
• Drag a handle past half a cell and the folder snaps between 1x1 and 2x2, with a little haptic tick. The side you pull decides the direction. A handle that can't go any further (an icon in the way, or the edge of the grid) just rubber-bands.
• The size button in the open folder's footer and the TalkBack action still work too.

3. <b>Folder animations that match the tile</b>
• Opening a large folder now grows the panel out of the 2x2 tile, and its apps fly from their spots on the tile to their places in the folder. Closing does the reverse, landing back on the tile.
• Switching between 1x1 and 2x2 morphs smoothly instead of snapping.
• Dragging a large folder shows the real tile at full size. When you hover an app over the tile, it springs and lights up the spot the app will take, and dropping it flies the app into that spot.
• Apps you open from the tile launch from their own spot where Android supports it.

4. <b>Preloaded font styles for everyone</b> (<a href="https://xdaforums.com/t/app-qa-android-17-expressive-launcher-pixel-inspired-customization-pixel-7-pro-feedback.4801789/post-90759508">XDA suggestion</a>)
• Settings > Icons & appearance > Font now opens for everyone, with eleven offline system styles (Sans, Medium, Condensed, Light, Black, Small Caps, Serif, Mono, Typewriter, Casual and Cursive) plus Google Sans Flex.
• Importing your own font files and browsing the Google Fonts catalog stay part of Expressive Pro.

5. <b>Safer Home screen loading</b>
• While building the widget overlap we found layouts where a folder and a widget sharing cells could be removed on the next restart. Loading now keeps both in every order, a folder that no longer fits becomes a normal 1x1 folder instead of disappearing, and a small folder never ends up hidden under a widget.
