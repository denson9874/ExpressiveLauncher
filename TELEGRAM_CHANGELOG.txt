<b>Expressive Launcher 5.1.4 (Build 76) — Color Without the Pack</b>

5.1.4 brings Monet-coloured stock icons to everyone on Android 13 and newer, no icon pack required. It also stops the launcher from undoing two choices you made elsewhere (a hidden status bar clock and your default Home app) and fixes a crash when folding a foldable.

───────────────────────

<b>✨ What's New &amp; Improved</b>

<b>1. 🎨 Themed icons without an icon pack</b>
• <b>Before</b>: the Themed icons setting stayed greyed out unless a themed icon pack (or Lawnicons) was installed. Even if you got it turned on, the launcher switched it back off at the next start when no pack was installed.
• <b>Now</b>: on Android 13 and newer, apps ship their own single-colour icon, so Themed icons works with stock icons and stays on. Settings &gt; Icons &amp; appearance &gt; Icon style &gt; Themed icons. Apps without a single-colour icon keep their normal colours. Android 12 still needs a themed icon pack.

<b>2. 🕒 A hidden status bar clock stays hidden</b>
• <b>Before</b>: if you hid the clock in Android's Status bar settings, the launcher brought it back after every reboot or launcher restart.
• <b>Now</b>: the launcher only brings back a clock that its own "Hide clock on the first home page" option hid.

<b>3. 🏠 The Home button respects your default launcher</b>
• <b>Before</b>: on ExpressiveOS, where Expressive Launcher is built in, pressing Home could open Expressive Launcher even after you chose another launcher as default.
• <b>Now</b>: Home goes to the launcher you picked. On a fresh ExpressiveOS install, Expressive Launcher is still the default.

<b>4. 📱 No crash when you fold a foldable</b>
• <b>Before</b>: folding a foldable after using it unfolded could crash the launcher. The cover screen has no taskbar, so the launcher worked out the taskbar icon scale from an icon size of 0.
• <b>Now</b>: the launcher skips the taskbar icon animation when there is no taskbar.

<b>5. 💳 Clearer Pro wording</b>
• The Pro sheet's button now says <b>Buy Pro · $4.99</b>, because it is a one-time purchase that unlocks Pro by itself, not a tip.
