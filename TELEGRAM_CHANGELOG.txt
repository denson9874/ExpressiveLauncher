<b>✨ Welcome to Version 3: The Next Chapter</b>

To ensure ongoing, independent development and long-term sustainability, Version 3 introduces our refined tier architecture:

• <b>Free Core (Free Forever &amp; Supported)</b>:
Choosing to remain on Free Core continues to receive regular updates, security patches, bug fixes, and 100% rock-solid Pixel Launcher parity with full Google Discover feed support. Free will always be maintained, privacy-first, and completely ad-free.

• <b>Expressive Pro (Deep Customization)</b>:
In-depth personalization features are now exclusive to Expressive Pro to support ongoing development:
  • <b>Workspace Grid Customization</b>: Custom column and row sliders, expanded foldable layouts, and dock icon counts.
  • <b>App Drawer Folders &amp; Tabs</b>: Custom drawer folders, category tabs, and drag-and-drop reordering.
  • <b>Custom Icon Shapes</b>: Custom corner radius editor with individual corner sculpting.
  • <b>Google &amp; Variable Fonts</b>: Curated Google Sans Flex variable font and extensive typography picker.
  • <b>Per-App Icon Overrides</b>: Independent icon pack selection for individual apps.

<b>🎁 Supporting Development &amp; Offline Keys</b>
