<b>Expressive Launcher 3.1.2 — FOSS Evolution, Obtainium & Streamlined Delivery</b>

Welcome to Expressive Launcher 3.1.2 (Build 44)! This release marks a pivotal evolution in how Expressive Launcher is delivered. We are embracing an open, friction-free FOSS distribution model—bringing first-class <b>Obtainium</b> background updates, <b>IzzyOnDroid / F-Droid</b> repository readiness, and complete transparency regarding our transition away from Google Play internal testing.

───────────────────────

<b>📢 Important Distribution Update: Transition Away from Google Play Testing</b>

To our testers and community members:
• <b>The Challenge with Google Play:</b> For personal developer accounts, Google Play Console enforces a policy requiring at least 20 testers opted-in continuously for 14 straight days before production access can even be reviewed. For a community-driven Android 17 launcher, this creates an artificial barrier and unnecessary delays. Furthermore, Google Play's billing policies conflict with our direct, privacy-respecting PayPal contributions and offline ECDSA P-256 Pro licensing.
• <b>The Open Resolution:</b> Moving forward, <b>GitHub Releases</b>, our built-in <b>In-App Updater</b>, and <b>Obtainium</b> are our primary, official distribution channels. You will receive updates the instant they are built, with zero wait times, zero store gatekeeping, and full feature preservation.

───────────────────────

<b>✨ What's New & Improved</b>

1. <b>FOSS & IzzyOnDroid / F-Droid Inclusion Readiness (Closes #22)</b>:
• Configured full standard Fastlane metadata (<code>fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/</code>) including titles, short/full descriptions, feature graphics, app icons, and per-version changelogs.
• Added <code>.fdroid.yml</code> in the repository root for automated crawler indexing, directly fulfilling community request #22 for open repository distribution.

2. <b>Native Obtainium 1-Click Background Updates</b>:
• Added direct 1-click Obtainium integration (<code>obtainium://add/https://github.com/denson9874/ExpressiveLauncher</code>) across the launcher:
• Under <b>Settings > About > Community</b>, tap <b>Add to Obtainium</b> to immediately configure seamless background updates.
• Built-in graceful web fallback: if Obtainium is not installed yet on your device, tapping the link opens the official Obtainium download page in your browser.
• Added Obtainium install badges and setup instructions to <code>README.md</code>.

3. <b>In-App Update Recovery & Fallback in Settings</b>:
• Enhanced <code>UpdateSection.kt</code> with smart action prompts: when the launcher is up to date or if a network check encounters a connection timeout, the launcher now provides a 1-tap button to launch or configure Obtainium.
• Preserves offline resilience and ensures you always have an automated update path even if GitHub raw CDNs experience caching delays.

4. <b>Streamlined Continuous Delivery Pipeline</b>:
• Decoupled heavy Google Play bundle uploads by default in our release orchestrator (<code>release_unified.py</code>), cutting release cycle times from ~8 minutes to under 2 minutes.
• Hardened Jenkins feed verification in <code>ci/publish_qa.py</code> with direct authenticated repository fallback when raw CDN caches take time to purge.
