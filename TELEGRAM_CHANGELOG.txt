<b>Expressive Launcher 5.0.9 (Build 71) — Badge of Honor</b>

5.0.9 is a small, focused release with two changes. On ExpressiveOS, Expressive's own ROM, every Pro feature is now included, with no key and no trial. Everywhere else, your work apps finally wear their badge in the app pickers. Pro, the Donate sheet and keys work exactly as before on every other ROM.

───────────────────────

<b>✨ What's New &amp; Improved</b>

<b>1. 🎁 Pro is included on ExpressiveOS</b>
• For phones running ExpressiveOS, where Expressive is the built-in Home screen.
• <b>Before</b>: Pro features were locked on ExpressiveOS like everywhere else, even though Expressive ships as part of the ROM.
• <b>Now</b>: every Pro feature is unlocked on ExpressiveOS. The Pro screen says "Included with ExpressiveOS". There's no trial card, no lock icons and no badges on Pro settings. Donating or entering a key is still there if you want to support development.
• Expressive recognises ExpressiveOS by the signing certificate of the phone's system itself, not by a setting or a system property, so root modules that hide or delete ROM properties don't switch Pro off. An update from GitHub or the in-app updater, installed over the built-in copy, keeps Pro.
• If you move your launcher backup to another ROM, Pro features lock again there and your settings stay saved.

<b>2. 💼 Work apps show their badge in the app pickers</b>
• <b>Before</b>: in Hidden apps, Dismissed predictions, the gesture "open app" picker, drawer-folder app selection and the Folder widget app picker, a work-profile app looked exactly like its personal copy. You saw two identical rows and had to guess which one you were hiding.
• <b>Now</b>: work, clone and private-space apps show the same small profile badge they have on Home and in search. Personal apps look as before.

<b>💬 Community Notes</b>
• <b>Who notices</b>: section 1 only matters on ExpressiveOS. Section 2 is for everyone with a work profile, a cloned app or a private space.
• <b>Why only two changes</b>: this release goes out early so ExpressiveOS phones get Pro without waiting for the next bigger update.
• <b>Still being worked on</b>:
    ◦ Custom folder icons (not just the Cover look) are on the list.
    ◦ Icon size differences between Home, the drawer and folders: if you see them, please post a screenshot with your grid, icon size and icon pack settings in the feedback group. On phones the two sizes share the same base, so we need your setup to find it.
• <b>Validation</b>: Jenkins passed 658 unit tests and 12 automated checks on an Android 17 emulator (details below). The ExpressiveOS check (Pro included) is covered by unit tests. It will be tried on a Pixel 7 Pro running ExpressiveOS once this update reaches it.
• <b>Developer Support</b>: Expressive Pro is available as a one-time $4.99 activation. Every dollar funds the build servers, test devices and signing pipeline that keep Expressive fast, independent and ad-free.
