<b>Expressive Launcher 5.1.1 (Build 73) — Left, Right, Your Call</b>

5.1.1 lets you flip the 3-button navigation bar. Android 17's switch that swaps back and recents now works with Expressive Launcher as your Recents app, the way it does with Pixel Launcher. It also gives small folders their names back so you can rename them, clears up the drawer blur after switching drawer styles, stops Smartspacer cards losing their first letters, and keeps Expressive Launcher as the default Home app on ExpressiveOS phones that also ship Pixel Launcher.

───────────────────────

<b>✨ What's New &amp; Improved</b>

<b>1. 🧭 The 3-button navigation order switch now works</b>
• <b>Before</b>: Android 17 can swap the 3-button bar to recents, home, back, but Expressive Launcher draws that bar and always used back, home, recents, so the switch did nothing.
• <b>Now</b>: the order follows the setting the moment you change it, in portrait, landscape and on a foldable's big screen. If you never touch the setting, nothing changes.

<b>2. 📁 Small folders can be renamed again</b>
• <b>Before</b>: a Home folder with 4 apps or fewer opened without its name, so there was nothing to tap to rename it. The folder's ⋮ button took just enough of the bottom row for the launcher to hide the name.
• <b>Now</b>: the ⋮ button no longer counts against the name, and a hidden name comes back once the folder has room. Tap the name to rename the folder, as before.

<b>3. 🌫️ The drawer blurs properly after you switch drawer styles</b>
• <b>Before</b>: switching the app drawer from full screen to bottom sheet could leave the sheet see-through with no blur, and a ghost of the drawer stayed on Home until the launcher restarted.
• <b>Now</b>: the launcher rebuilds its layout when you switch, checks itself for a stale layout when it comes back, and only shows Home behind the sheet when blur is actually on.

<b>4. 🌤️ Smartspacer cards keep their first letters</b>
• <b>Before</b>: on short At a Glance rows, Smartspacer's cards slid left past the edge, so "Wed, Nov 4" read "Ved, Nov 4".
• <b>Now</b>: the cards sit inside the row's padding, like the built-in At a Glance.

<b>5. 🏠 ExpressiveOS keeps Expressive Launcher as the default Home</b>
• <b>Before</b>: if a phone shipped Expressive Launcher and Pixel Launcher side by side, neither won by default, so a clean install would ask which Home app to use.
• <b>Now</b>: on ExpressiveOS, Expressive Launcher is the default Home and Pixel Launcher stays available in Default apps. It changes nothing on other phones.
