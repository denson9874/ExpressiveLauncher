<b>Expressive Launcher 4.0.2 QA — Think Big, Fold Bigger</b>

Welcome to Expressive Launcher 4.0.2 (Build 51)! You asked for large folders, so Home screen folders can now grow to 2x2 and open apps straight from the tile. At a Glance can also show a short daily message under the date, and this build fixes the three search issues still listed after 4.0.1.

───────────────────────

<b>✨ What's New & Improved</b>

1. <b>Large 2x2 Home screen folders</b> (<a href="https://xdaforums.com/t/app-qa-android-17-expressive-launcher-looking-for-pixel-11-pro-pro-xl-feedback.4801792/post-90744865">XDA request</a>)
• <b>How:</b> open a folder on the Home screen and tap the new size button in its footer (TalkBack users get a <i>Make folder large</i> action). The folder grows into the free spaces next to it, to the right and down first. If there's no room, it tells you instead of moving your icons.
• <b>What you get:</b> a rounded tile showing the first four apps as full-size icons. Tap one to open that app directly; tap anywhere else to open the folder. With more than four apps, the last slot previews the rest. Drop an app anywhere on the tile to add it.
• <b>Safe by design:</b> <i>Make folder small</i> puts it back to 1x1 in its top-left cell. If a large folder ever doesn't fit after a grid change, it loads as a normal folder instead of disappearing. Dock and app drawer folders stay 1x1.

2. <b>Daily messages in At a Glance</b>
• A new <b>Daily messages</b> switch under <i>At a Glance > What to show</i> (off by default) adds one short line after the weather on the date card, such as <i>74°F · Progress, not perfection</i>.
• Quotes and encouraging words alternate each day, and common holidays get their own greeting. Easter, Mother's Day, Father's Day and Thanksgiving are worked out for each year, and holidays specific to one country only show in that region.

3. <b>Search on Google shows the right icon</b>
• <b>What went wrong:</b> action rows such as <i>Search on Google</i> sometimes flashed the previous row's icon (often the Play Store) until the real one finished loading behind the search.
• <b>Now:</b> fixed icons (the web search provider, suggestions, history, calculator, app shortcuts) are remembered and appear in the same frame as the row. Result icons no longer wait about 200 ms behind the search.

4. <b>No blank cards when results appear</b>
• <b>What went wrong:</b> as the first results faded in, their rounded cards were drawn at full strength around still-invisible rows for a few frames, so you saw empty cards.
• <b>Now:</b> the cards fade and scale together with their rows.

5. <b>TalkBack keeps app names after a language change</b>
• <b>What went wrong:</b> after switching the system language and back, TalkBack read most drawer apps without a name (16 of 21 on our test emulator).
• <b>Now:</b> every drawer app keeps its spoken name after the switch (21 of 21).
