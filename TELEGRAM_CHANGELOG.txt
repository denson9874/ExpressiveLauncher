<b>✨ What's New in Version 3.0.2</b>

• <b>Automated Donation &amp; In-App Activation ($4.99)</b>:
  You can now support ongoing independent development directly from within the launcher preferences! 
  • Supports 1-tap contribution via PayPal.
  • Your privacy-preserving installation Device ID is linked automatically.
  • Tap <b>"Check Activation Status"</b> to instantly verify and unlock Expressive Pro on your device—no manual key copy-pasting required!
  • Includes manual transaction ID verification fallback if needed.

• <b>Polished Settings &amp; Pro UI</b>:
  • <b>Standardized Pro Banner</b>: The Pro status card in Settings now uses standard preference group styling, matching list item margins, rounded corners, and vertical rhythm perfectly.
  • <b>Normalized Pro Badges</b>: Pro badges across Dock, Folders, and Gestures now use a standardized 20dp height and align inline directly beside category titles, keeping list rows and trailing edges completely uniform.

• <b>Free Core &amp; Parity Maintained</b>:
  • Free Core remains supported, ad-free, and privacy-respecting forever with 100% Google Discover feed parity.
  • All community tester, VIP, and giveaway offline keys remain 100% active and supported!
