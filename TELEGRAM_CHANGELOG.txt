<b>Expressive Launcher QA 2.0.12 (31) — Hardware Bitmap Software Safety Hotfix</b>

Welcome to <b>Expressive Launcher QA 2.0.12</b>! This hotfix resolves a critical software rendering crash encountered on physical Android 17 devices when opening the icon customization dialog for themed and cached icons.

───────────────────────

<b>🚀 What's New & Fixed</b>
• <b>Software Rendering Crash Resolution</b>:
• Addressed an uncaught <code>IllegalArgumentException: Software rendering doesn't support hardware bitmaps</code> on physical Android 17 devices (Pixel 11 Pro XL) when opening <code>CustomizeDialog</code>.
• Jetpack Compose's <code>rememberDrawablePainter</code> renders icons onto an internal software canvas. Upstream themed icons and GPU-allocated bitmaps with <code>Bitmap.Config.HARDWARE</code> triggered Android framework's strict software canvas rejection.
• Implemented <code>ensureSoftwareRendering()</code> to safely inspect and convert hardware bitmaps within <code>AdaptiveIconDrawable</code>, <code>InsetDrawable</code>, <code>DrawableWrapper</code>, <code>BitmapDrawable</code>, and <code>FastBitmapDrawable</code> to software-compatible <code>ARGB_8888</code> copies before drawing.
• Prevents crashes when customizing app icons or pinned shortcuts on home screen.
