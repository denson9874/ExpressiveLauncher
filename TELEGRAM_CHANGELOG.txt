<b>Expressive Launcher 3.1.6 QA — Search and Ye Shall Find</b>

Welcome to Expressive Launcher 3.1.6 (Build 48)! Settings search used to answer "No widgets or shortcuts found" when you asked it for a setting. This build teaches it to find every setting, walk you to the row and knock twice.

───────────────────────

<b>✨ What's New & Improved</b>

1. <b>Settings search finds every setting</b>
• <b>What went wrong:</b> the search list was a short hand-written set of English phrases that had drifted from the screens. Typing <i>return</i>, <i>suggestions in dock</i>, <i>status bar</i>, <i>Discover</i> or <i>rotation</i> found nothing or the wrong thing, and an empty result said "No widgets or shortcuts found" (the widget picker's message). It even listed settings Expressive doesn't have.
• <b>Now:</b> about 150 settings are searchable, in your language, and each result opens the screen it lives on. The best match comes first: an exact title, then titles that start with your words, then the rest. Accents and capitals don't matter, and every word you type has to match.
• The search box now says <b>Search settings</b> and an empty result says <b>No matching settings</b>.

2. <b>Search takes you to the setting</b>
• Choose a result and the screen scrolls to that row, with its neighbours in view, and pulses it twice so you can spot it.
• Options hidden behind an Expressive Pro lock stay hidden; you land on the lock card.

3. <b>TalkBack can name Settings' icon buttons</b>
• Back, <b>Search settings</b>, <b>More options</b>, <b>Clear</b>, copy, paste, reset and download buttons were all announced as just "Button". They now say what they do, and any new icon button has to carry a label.

4. <b>One less switch that did nothing</b>
• <b>Live information</b> in <i>Icons & appearance</i> promised announcements and default feature flags, but Expressive never fetched or showed either. The switch and the empty <b>Updates</b> heading above it are gone.

5. <b>Clearer Discover setup</b>
• If Android answers "App not installed" because an old Expressive Feed is still hiding in Private space or a work profile, the message now names that as the likely cause. Android hides that copy from apps, so this can only be explained after the attempt.

───────────────────────

<b>🗣️ From Your Reports</b>
• <b>Finding settings</b> (the navigation and discoverability threads): search is now a dependable way in, and it takes you to the row instead of to the top of a long page.
• <b>Return to default page (XDA-015):</b> we re-tested this on 3.1.5. With the switch off, opening an app from page 2 and coming back with Back or Home leaves you on page 2. With it on (the default, as on Pixel), you return to page 1. No change needed.
