<b>Expressive Launcher 4.1.0 (Build 59) — Now You See It, Now You Don't</b>

Three improvements straight from community feedback in our Telegram group. Thank you for the detailed list!

<b>✨ What's New & Improved</b>

1. <b>One-tap Hide in the app drawer</b>:
• Long-press any app in the app drawer (or its suggestions row) and choose <b>Hide</b>. It disappears from the drawer immediately.
• Changed your mind? Tap <b>Undo</b> on the bar that appears at the bottom of the screen.
• Before, hiding took three taps through Customize. Hide only appears for apps opened from the drawer, and never while the Home screen is locked.

2. <b>No more accidental folder-to-widget conversions</b>:
• Inside an open Home folder, the button next to the folder name is now a <b>⋮ menu</b>. <b>Make widget</b> (or <b>Make normal folder</b> for a Folder widget) lives inside it, so converting always takes a deliberate second tap.

3. <b>Transparent search bar while scrolling</b> (new option):
• New switch in <b>Settings → App list → Transparent search bar while scrolling</b>.
• When it's on, the area behind the app drawer search bar stays see-through as you scroll, instead of filling in with a solid band. The search field keeps its own background so it stays easy to read. Off by default.

<blockquote>😄 Your apps can now play hide-and-seek. Unlike real hide-and-seek, there's an Undo button for when nobody can find them.

📁 Folders were getting promoted to widgets without asking. They now need a ⋮ second opinion.</blockquote>
