<b>Expressive Launcher 5.0.1 (Build 63) — No Place Like Home</b>

5.0.1 is a "stay Home" release. It fixes two ways Home could crash, and two ways it could quietly lose your setup. Nothing flashy, just a launcher that keeps its footing. If your Home screen got stuck crashing after you renamed a shortcut, this is the update for you.

───────────────────────

<b>✨ What's New & Improved</b>

<b>1. 🔁 Renaming a shortcut no longer traps Home in a crash loop</b>
• <b>The bug</b>: Renaming an <b>Open app</b> action shortcut, the kind you add from <b>Widgets → Expressive Launcher L3 → Actions</b>, saved a name Home couldn't read back. From then on, Home crashed every time it started, until you cleared the app's data.
• <b>The fix</b>: Home now reads those names correctly, so your renamed shortcut keeps its new name. If a saved name can't be read, Home skips it instead of crashing. On first start, 5.0.1 also repairs names that 5.0.0 saved.
• <b>Already stuck in the loop?</b> Install 5.0.1 from GitHub or Obtainium; the in-app updater can't help while Home keeps crashing. You don't need to clear data.

<b>2. 💾 Restores keep your layout, and backups keep your latest changes</b>
• <b>Wallpaper-only restore</b>: Restoring a backup with only <b>Wallpaper</b> selected used to delete your Home layout, custom icons, drawer folders and folder-widget styles along the way. Now it restores just the wallpaper, as it says.
• <b>Fresher backups</b>: A custom icon, drawer folder or folder-widget style you changed just before making a backup could be missing from it. Backups now include those latest changes too.

<b>3. ♿ Accessibility tools no longer crash Home around widgets</b>
• If a widget disappeared while its resize handles were open (for example, its app was disabled), the handles stayed on screen. An accessibility service such as TalkBack, an automation app or a password manager could then crash Home just by inspecting them. The handles now close with the widget, and Home ignores resize requests for a widget that's gone.

<b>4. 📱 Widgets open on tablets and unfolded foldables</b>
• On tablets and unfolded foldables, opening <b>Widgets</b> crashed Home: right away if you have a work profile, or as soon as suggestions loaded if you don't. Both two-pane layouts are fixed, so the picker opens with its Suggestions, Personal and Work tabs.

<b>5. 📣 Announcements link updated</b>
• <b>Settings → About</b> now opens our announcements channel at its new home, <a href="https://t.me/ExpressiveGroup">@ExpressiveGroup</a>.

<b>💬 Community Notes</b>
• <b>Still being worked on</b> (see the 5.0.0 release notes for workarounds):
• Home can stop updating after a dark-mode or font change. Restart Home from <b>⋮</b> in Home settings.
• Swiping up on a cover folder doesn't open it yet; tap still opens the cover app.
• The headphone battery card in At a Glance doesn't appear yet.
• Lowering Dock pages or Dock rows, or folding a foldable, can remove dock icons that no longer fit.
• <b>Validation</b>: 565 automated tests pass. The fixes were checked on Android 17 (QPR2) emulators: a Pixel phone and a tablet, with and without a work profile. Physical-device reports are very welcome.
