# Expressive Launcher 5.1.2 (Build 74) — Sized Right, Started Right

5.1.2 is a small, report-driven update. Changing the Home icon size now applies the moment you move the slider, with no launcher restart. On Android 12 to 15, phones where QuickSwitch makes Expressive Launcher the Recents app no longer crash on every start; Recents through QuickSwitch now needs Android 16 or newer. And Expressive Core users can now tap the notification dots setting to go straight to the matching Expressive Full download.

---

### ✨ What's New & Improved

#### 1. 📐 Home icon size changes apply right away
- **Before**: changing the Home icon size sometimes rebuilt Home, the dock and folders at the old size, and the new size only showed up after the launcher restarted.
- **Now**: the launcher reads the size you just saved before it redraws Home. In testing, eight changes in a row each applied straight away, with no restart. Every other setting that redraws Home or restarts the launcher benefits from the same fix.

#### 2. 📱 No more crash loop with QuickSwitch on Android 12 to 15
- **Before**: with Expressive Launcher set as the Recents app through QuickSwitch, Android 12 crashed the launcher on every start. The Recents and gesture code relies on parts of Android that first appeared in Android 16, so it could not work on older versions.
- **Now**: Recents through QuickSwitch needs Android 16 or newer, and QuickSwitch is told so. On Android 12 to 15 the launcher switches off its Recents service, keeps working as your Home app, and shows a notice that says so. Until you pick another Recents app in QuickSwitch, swipe-up Home and the Recents button don't work there, but Back and the Home button do. Android 16 and newer are unchanged.

#### 3. 🔔 Expressive Core links straight to Expressive Full
- **Before**: Core's notification dots setting said to install Expressive Full but didn't say where to get it.
- **Now**: tap the setting to open the Expressive Full download for the same version. Full installs over Core and keeps your setup. If Play Protect blocks Full on your phone, Core is still the version that installs.
