# Expressive Launcher 3.1.4 QA — Quiet Dock, Solid Top

Welcome to Expressive Launcher 3.1.4 (Build 46)! This build is about the edges of your Home screen. The dock stops filling empty spots with apps you didn't ask for, the status bar can get a solid background, and Dock settings are easier to find. All three come from the Pixel 11 Pro XDA thread. Thank you, nutzfreelance, for the detailed list.

---

### ✨ What's New & Improved

1. **Suggestions in dock toggle (XDA-004)**
   - New **Suggestions in dock** switch in **Home screen**, **Dock** and **Suggestions** settings. It works like Pixel's "suggestions on Home screen" option.
   - Turn it off and empty dock spots stay empty. Predicted apps leave the dock right away, with no restart.
   - Turning it off only hides suggestions. It never restores an old dock backup or moves your pinned apps, and switching it back on shows the latest suggestions immediately.
   - App drawer suggestions have their own switch and are not affected.

2. **Solid status bar background (XDA-010)**
   - New **Home screen > Status bar > Solid status bar background** switch paints a black bar behind the clock and icons on Home.
   - It covers exactly the status bar area and is only drawn while the status bar is shown. It fades out with the rest of the Home chrome when you open the app drawer or Recents.
   - While it is on, status bar icons stay light so they remain readable on the dark bar, even with a light wallpaper or *Dark status bar icons* turned on.
   - Android controls the status bar's height, so a Home app can't change it. The setting says so rather than promise a height control.

3. **Dock settings from Home screen settings (XDA-012)**
   - **Home screen** settings now has a **Dock** row that opens the full Dock page (search bar, icon count, background and suggestions), so you don't have to know it sits under **Advanced**.
   - The dashboard's Advanced section is unchanged, so existing shortcuts and muscle memory still work.

Also confirmed for **XDA-005**: the app drawer icon requested for the dock has shipped since 2.0.10. Use **Home screen > Add app drawer icon** or **Dock > Add app drawer icon**.
