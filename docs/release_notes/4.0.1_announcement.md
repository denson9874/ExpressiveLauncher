# Expressive Launcher 4.0.1 QA — Enter the Dragon (Just Once)

Welcome to Expressive Launcher 4.0.1 (Build 50)! 4.0.0 made app search quicker. It turned out Enter was sometimes quicker still: pressed right after the last letter, it arrived before the results and got ignored. On some keyboards a single press also arrived twice. This build fixes both, and gives the app drawer a solid background when Battery Saver switches blur off.

---

### ✨ What's New & Improved

1. **Enter right after typing opens what you typed**
   - **What went wrong:** search results show up a moment after each letter. If you pressed Enter inside that moment, Expressive had no result for your query yet and did nothing. If results for a shorter query were still on screen (*wea* while you typed *weat*), Enter could open one of those instead.
   - **Now:** an early Enter waits for your query's results (up to 2 seconds) and opens the top result as soon as it shows. Results left over from fewer letters are never opened.

2. **One Enter, one web search**
   - **What went wrong:** hardware keyboards and some on-screen keyboards deliver Enter as a key press *and* a search action. Both could start the web search, so the browser opened twice.
   - **Now:** a second Enter for the same query within 0.8 seconds of opening something is ignored. Pressing Enter again later still works.

3. **Solid app drawer in Battery Saver** ([XDA feedback](https://xdaforums.com/t/app-qa-android-17-expressive-launcher-pixel-inspired-customization-pixel-7-pro-feedback.4801789/post-90758080))
   - **What went wrong:** the drawer sheet sits on a blur. When Android switches blur off (Battery Saver, overheating or reduced transparency), the sheet kept the *App drawer opacity* value, 50% by default, so Home screen icons showed through the app grid.
   - **Now:** without blur the sheet uses a solid Material You color, and it switches back as soon as blur returns. With blur on, the opacity slider works as before.

---

### 🗣️ From Your Reports

- **Blur and Battery Saver (XDA, Pixel 7 Pro thread):** the app drawer now turns solid when blur is off. Recents already blurs the wallpaper wherever Android allows blur, and uses its own non-blurred style when Android turns blur off, so we left Recents unchanged. If Recents shows no blur on your phone with Battery Saver off, please tell us your device and Android build.
- **Known search gaps from 4.0.0:** two of the five are fixed here (the ignored Enter and the double web search). The Play Store icon flicker on *Search on Google*, the brief blank result cards and the missing TalkBack names after a language change are still on the list.

### 🧪 Validation

- App unit suite: 390 tests passing, including 9 new ones: 7 for when Enter launches, waits or is ignored (`SearchSubmitGateTest`) and 2 for the drawer sheet opacity with and without blur (`AllAppsSheetAlphaTest`).
- CI pipeline contract tests: 291 passing.
- Android 17 QPR2 Beta 5 emulator, debug build of this source: typing *clock* and pressing Enter at once opened Clock exactly once, although the search box received two search actions 2 ms apart. The released 4.0.0 opened nothing for the same input. A web-only query opened Chrome once from the launcher. With the emulator on battery and Battery Saver on, Android reported blur off and the drawer turned solid right away. Battery Saver and the battery state were reset afterwards.
- Jenkins handles the signed QA build, emulator smoke checks and publication. No physical-device matrix is claimed for this build.
