# Expressive Launcher 3.1.1 — Quickstep Android 17 Fix & 24/7 Activation Cloud

Welcome to Expressive Launcher 3.1.1 (Build 43)! This release delivers critical compatibility updates for Android 17 Quickstep gestures, connects to our live 24/7 serverless activation cloud backend, and hardens recents gesture transitions.

---

### ✨ What's New & Improved

1. **Android 17 Quickstep & QuickSwitch Support (Closes #24)**:
   - Resolved a fatal `NoSuchMethodError` (`createInputConsumer`) in `InputConsumerController.java`, `TouchInteractionService.java`, and `PipInputConsumer.java` when enabling Quickstep recents via QuickSwitch on Pixel 9 Pro and Android 17 devices.
   - Replaced fragile platform interface assumptions with safe, multi-platform linkage guards (`LinkageError | RemoteException | RuntimeException`) and safe input channel lifecycles.

2. **24/7 Serverless Pro Activation Backend**:
   - Deployed and synchronized our live global edge worker backend on Cloudflare Workers (`https://expressive-pro-activation.daryldenson-apps.workers.dev/`).
   - Seamlessly verifies PayPal contributions in real time with high-speed key-value storage. Tapping **"Check Payment Status"** now retrieves and activates your Pro license in under 2 seconds worldwide—completely independent of local developer machines.

3. **Multi-Window & Desktop Transition Stability**:
   - Added null-safety guards in `AbsSwipeUpHandler.java` for `mRecentsView` during desktop windowing and swipe-up transitions.
   - Prevents race conditions during rapid gesture cancellation or navigation mode switches.
