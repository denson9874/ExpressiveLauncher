# Expressive Launcher 5.1.5 (Build 79) — Your Drawer, Your Order

5.1.5 lets you choose how the app drawer is sorted, puts a lock on Hidden apps, and gives you more control over how the wallpaper scrolls. At a Glance can now fall back to backup weather, and foldables get the right navigation bar after folding.

---

### ✨ What's New & Improved

#### 1. 🔤 Sort the app drawer your way
- **Before**: the app drawer was always alphabetical.
- **Now**: Settings > App drawer > Sort apps offers **Alphabetical**, **Newest installed first** and **Most used first**. Most used ranks apps by their time on screen over the last 30 days and updates when you return Home. It needs Usage access, and the launcher asks for it when you pick that option. Apps with no ranking stay alphabetical below the rest.

#### 2. 🔒 Hidden apps stay hidden
- **Before**: anyone holding your unlocked phone could open Settings > App drawer > Hidden apps and see what you hid.
- **Now**: the page asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock first, and asks again each time you open it. Phones without a screen lock open it straight away. Hidden apps is a Pro feature.

#### 3. 🖼️ More wallpaper control
- **Scroll across the whole wallpaper**: with 2 or 3 home screen pages, swiping now moves from one edge of the wallpaper to the other, instead of stopping partway. Settings > Home screen > Wallpaper, shown while Wallpaper scrolling is on.
- **Center wallpaper on one page**: with a single home screen page, show the middle of the wallpaper instead of its left edge.
- Both are off by default.

#### 4. 🌦️ Backup weather for At a Glance
- **Before**: when Google's At a Glance stopped showing weather, the date card had none.
- **Now**: turn on **Backup weather** (At a Glance > What to show) to show current conditions from MET Norway while Google has none. It uses your approximate location, rounded before it leaves the phone, and Google's weather takes over again as soon as it's back. Off by default.

#### 5. 📱 Foldables: the right bars after folding
- **Before**: folding a foldable could leave the cover screen with the inner screen's layout: no phone navigation bar on Home and a large taskbar inside apps, until the launcher restarted.
- **Now**: the launcher checks the screen layout again after a fold and rebuilds the navigation bar and taskbar to match.
