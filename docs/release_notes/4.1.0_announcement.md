# Expressive Launcher 4.1.0 (Build 59) — Now You See It, Now You Don't

Three improvements straight from community feedback in our Telegram group. Thank you for the detailed list!

### ✨ What's New & Improved

1. **One-tap Hide in the app drawer**:
   - Long-press any app in the app drawer (or its suggestions row) and choose **Hide**. It disappears from the drawer immediately.
   - Changed your mind? Tap **Undo** on the bar that appears at the bottom of the screen.
   - Before, hiding took three taps through Customize. Hide only appears for apps opened from the drawer, and never while the Home screen is locked.

2. **No more accidental folder-to-widget conversions**:
   - Inside an open Home folder, the button next to the folder name is now a **⋮ menu**. **Make widget** (or **Make normal folder** for a Folder widget) lives inside it, so converting always takes a deliberate second tap.

3. **Transparent search bar while scrolling** (new option):
   - New switch in **Settings → App list → Transparent search bar while scrolling**.
   - When it's on, the area behind the app drawer search bar stays see-through as you scroll, instead of filling in with a solid band. The search field keeps its own background so it stays easy to read. Off by default.

> 😄 Your apps can now play hide-and-seek. Unlike real hide-and-seek, there's an Undo button for when nobody can find them.
>
> 📁 Folders were getting promoted to widgets without asking. They now need a ⋮ second opinion.
