# Expressive Launcher 3.1.2 — FOSS Evolution, Obtainium & Streamlined Delivery

Welcome to Expressive Launcher 3.1.2 (Build 44)! This release marks a pivotal evolution in how Expressive Launcher is delivered. We are embracing an open, friction-free FOSS distribution model—bringing first-class **Obtainium** background updates, **IzzyOnDroid / F-Droid** repository readiness, and complete transparency regarding our transition away from Google Play internal testing.

---

### 📢 Important Distribution Update: Transition Away from Google Play Testing

To our testers and community members:
- **The Challenge with Google Play:** For personal developer accounts, Google Play Console enforces a policy requiring at least 20 testers opted-in continuously for 14 straight days before production access can even be reviewed. For a community-driven Android 17 launcher, this creates an artificial barrier and unnecessary delays. Furthermore, Google Play's billing policies conflict with our direct, privacy-respecting PayPal contributions and offline ECDSA P-256 Pro licensing.
- **The Open Resolution:** Moving forward, **GitHub Releases**, our built-in **In-App Updater**, and **Obtainium** are our primary, official distribution channels. You will receive updates the instant they are built, with zero wait times, zero store gatekeeping, and full feature preservation.

---

### ✨ What's New & Improved

1. **FOSS & IzzyOnDroid / F-Droid Inclusion Readiness (Closes #22)**:
   - Configured full standard Fastlane metadata (`fastlane/metadata/android/en-US/`) including titles, short/full descriptions, feature graphics, app icons, and per-version changelogs.
   - Added `.fdroid.yml` in the repository root for automated crawler indexing, directly fulfilling community request #22 for open repository distribution.

2. **Native Obtainium 1-Click Background Updates**:
   - Added direct 1-click Obtainium integration (`obtainium://add/https://github.com/denson9874/ExpressiveLauncher`) across the launcher:
     - Under **Settings > About > Community**, tap **Add to Obtainium** to immediately configure seamless background updates.
     - Built-in graceful web fallback: if Obtainium is not installed yet on your device, tapping the link opens the official Obtainium download page in your browser.
   - Added Obtainium install badges and setup instructions to `README.md`.

3. **In-App Update Recovery & Fallback in Settings**:
   - Enhanced `UpdateSection.kt` with smart action prompts: when the launcher is up to date or if a network check encounters a connection timeout, the launcher now provides a 1-tap button to launch or configure Obtainium.
   - Preserves offline resilience and ensures you always have an automated update path even if GitHub raw CDNs experience caching delays.

4. **Streamlined Continuous Delivery Pipeline**:
   - Decoupled heavy Google Play bundle uploads by default in our release orchestrator (`release_unified.py`), cutting release cycle times from ~8 minutes to under 2 minutes.
   - Hardened Jenkins feed verification in `ci/publish_qa.py` with direct authenticated repository fallback when raw CDN caches take time to purge.
