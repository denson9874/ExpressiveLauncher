# Expressive Launcher 5.1.7 (Build 80) — Your Photo, Your Icon

5.1.7 lets you turn any picture into an app icon, hide the small app badge on shortcut icons, and open Google's Create My Widget from the widget picker. Backup weather now shows the temperature unit you chose on your phone.

---

### ✨ What's New & Improved

#### 1. 🖼️ Make an app icon from your own image
- **Before**: the icon picker only offered icons from icon packs.
- **Now**: pick **From an image** in the icon picker, choose a photo with Android's photo picker (no storage permission needed), then drag and pinch to frame a square. The icon takes your icon shape and keeps working after the photo is deleted. Layout backups carry your image icons. This is a Pro feature, like the other custom icons.

#### 2. 🔘 Hide the app badge on shortcut icons
- **Before**: shortcuts an app adds to Home always carried a small round icon of that app.
- **Now**: turn off **Show app badge on shortcuts** in Settings > Icons & appearance to remove it from pinned shortcuts, the long-press list and the drag preview. It applies at once and is on by default.

#### 3. 🌡️ Backup weather in your temperature unit
- **Before**: Backup weather picked Fahrenheit or Celsius from the phone's country alone, so a phone set to English (United States) showed Fahrenheit even with Celsius chosen.
- **Now**: it follows the temperature you chose under Settings > System > Languages > Regional preferences, and only falls back to the country's habit when you chose none.

#### 4. 🧩 Create My Widget from the widget picker
- **Before**: the widget picker had no way into Google's Create My Widget.
- **Now**: on Pixels that have Create My Widget, a **Create** button sits at the top of the widget picker. Build your widget there and it is added to Home when you finish. The button only shows when the phone has an app that offers it.
