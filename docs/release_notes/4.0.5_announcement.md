# Expressive Launcher 4.0.5 (Build 54)

### ✨ What's New & Improved

1. **Work Profile Tab Alignment & Layout Fixes**
   - Fixed a critical layout regression where the **Personal** and **Work** profile tab strip could be translated down by hundreds of pixels directly over app icons.
   - Fixed an issue where closing the app drawer while scrolled caused the profile tabs to remain in a collapsed state and fail to reload when reopening the drawer.
   - Guaranteed that whenever the app drawer is at the top, the Personal/Work tab header is immediately visible and aligned at zero translation.

2. **Edge-to-Edge Full-Screen App Drawer**
   - Restored standard edge-to-edge full-screen App Drawer presentation on all handheld devices, disabling the experimental bottom sheet presentation that introduced top pull handles (`—`) and sheet margin cutoffs.

3. **Fast Scroll Synchronization & Stability**
   - Restored pure Launcher3 fast scroll synchronization and position reset mechanics, eliminating invalid scroll coordinate type conversions and ensuring clean, instantaneous returns to the top of the app list.
