# Expressive Launcher 3.1.5 QA — Swipe Right, Search Bright

Welcome to Expressive Launcher 3.1.5 (Build 47)! This build fixes Google Discover and brings two Android 17 QPR2 touches to Home: Google's own search bar in the dock and Pixel's new long-press menu.

---

### ✨ What's New & Improved

1. **Google Discover works again**
   - **What went wrong:** if Expressive didn't have install permission yet, setup offered the standalone *Expressive Feed Companion* download. That APK was signed for the retired Google Play edition and refuses to connect to Expressive from GitHub. Discover stayed blank while settings said it was on, and Android wouldn't let the built-in helper replace it.
   - **Set up Google Discover** is now always shown. It installs the Discover helper included with Expressive. When Android asks, choose **Always allow** for Expressive under *Install unknown apps*; *Ask every time* isn't enough. If Play Protect shows **App scan recommended**, tap **Scan app**, then **Install**.
   - **Installed the old companion?** Settings now offer **Replace Discover support**. It opens Android's uninstall confirmation, then installs the built-in helper.
   - Discover support is now FOSS-only. The standalone companion is retired, its download page explains the built-in setup, and the helper only trusts the launcher it shipped with.

2. **Google's own search bar in the dock**
   - Like Pixel Launcher on Android 17 QPR2, the dock now hosts the Google app's own search widget, with search, voice and Lens. The buttons come from the Google app, so AI Mode appears when Google offers it for your account.
   - Android asks once whether Expressive may create the widget. Tap **Create**.
   - Fixed an empty bar: the Google search bar option picked a Google widget that draws nothing in the dock.
   - If you chose a different dock bar, you keep it. Without the Google app, the dock keeps Expressive's own bar.

3. **Android 17 QPR2 home menu**
   - Long-press Home for one wallpaper-tinted card instead of separate tiles, with filled icons and a gear for **Home settings**.
   - The top row shows your current wallpaper, followed by up to three recent ones. Tap the current one to open **Wallpaper & style**.
   - The menu keeps the same card if you turn the wallpaper row off.

For developers: debug and release builds can be installed side by side again.
