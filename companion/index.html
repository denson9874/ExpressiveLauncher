⚠️ Android / Chrome Download Troubleshooting:
  • "File might be harmful" prompt: Tap "Download anyway". Android displays this warning for all APKs downloaded outside the Play Store.
  • Download seems paused or stuck: Open the Files by Google (or your device's File Manager) > Downloads folder. If you see ExpressiveFeed.apk.crdownload, Chrome has finished the transfer and just needs you to tap it or refresh the browser.
  • In-App Browsers (ChatGPT, Reddit, Telegram): Tap the three dots (⋮) in the top right corner and tap "Open in Chrome" or your default browser.
📲 How to Install & Enable
  1. Tap the Download button above to download ExpressiveFeed.apk.
  2. Tap the finished download notification to install it (enable Allow from this source if prompted).
  3. Open Expressive Launcher > Home Settings > Google Discover.
  4. Switch on Show Google Discover.
  5. Return to your Home screen and swipe right to see your Google Discover feed!
